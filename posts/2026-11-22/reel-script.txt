McDonald's collected over ten billion dollars in rent in twenty twenty-four. Rent, not burgers. In nineteen fifty-six, the company set up a real estate arm to buy land and lease it to its franchisees. Today they pay McDonald's rent, plus royalties on their sales. That rent alone was nearly forty percent of the company's total revenue. McDonald's isn't just selling you fries. It's its franchisees' landlord. Own the ground under the business, and you win either way. Which would you rather own, the restaurant or the land? Follow Behind The Brand for more.
