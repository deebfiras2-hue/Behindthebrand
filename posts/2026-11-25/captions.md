## Carousel (6 slides, photo cover) - Spanx / Sara Blakely
She started Spanx with $5,000 and never took an outside investor. Then she became a billionaire 🧦

Sara Blakely was a fax machine saleswoman with no fashion or business training. She cut the feet off control-top pantyhose to solve her own problem.
She got ten minutes with Neiman Marcus buyers and paid her own flight to pitch them.
In 2012, Forbes named her the youngest self-made female billionaire. In 2021, Blackstone bought a majority stake at a $1.2 billion valuation.

Would you rather have a big check or full control of your company? 👇
