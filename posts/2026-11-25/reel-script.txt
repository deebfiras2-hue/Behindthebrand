Google pays Apple about twenty billion dollars a year. Not for ads. Not for tech. Just to be the default search on your iPhone. Court filings put it at twenty billion in twenty twenty-two. And Google's CEO confirmed in court that Google shares thirty-six percent of its Safari search ad revenue with Apple. Why pay so much? Because the default is the front door... and everything behind it is search ads. A judge ruled in twenty twenty-five that the payments can continue, just not as exclusive deals. The lesson?
