Ryanair sells you a flight for about forty-six euros. [short pause] But that's not where it makes its money. In the year to March twenty twenty-five, extras like bags, seats and priority boarding brought in four point seven billion euros. That's about a third of all its revenue... and almost three times its profit of one point six billion. Gate staff have even been offered a bonus for spotting oversized bags. The lesson?
