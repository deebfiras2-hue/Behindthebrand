## Carousel (7 slides, photo cover) - Blockbuster / Netflix
Blockbuster could have bought Netflix for $50 million. Netflix's founders say it said no 🎬

In 2000, Netflix's co-founders say they offered to sell to Blockbuster for $50 million. Reed Hastings' book says CEO John Antioco declined; Antioco has disputed that serious talks took place.
Blockbuster was making about $800 million a year from late fees, roughly 16% of its revenue. Netflix's subscription had none.
Blockbuster filed for bankruptcy in September 2010. By the end of 2024, Netflix reported over 300 million paid memberships.

Would you have bought Netflix for $50 million? 👇
