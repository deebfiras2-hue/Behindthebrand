Disney isn't really a movie company. In fiscal twenty twenty-five, its theme parks, cruises and merchandise made ten billion dollars in operating profit. That's about fifty-seven percent of everything Disney earned. Its whole film, TV and streaming business made less than half of that... four point seven billion. The movies build the characters. The parks sell them back to you, at a premium, every single day. The lesson? Let your hits feed the thing that really pays. Would you rather own the studio, or the theme park?
