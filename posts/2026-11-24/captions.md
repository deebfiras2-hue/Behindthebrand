## Carousel (6 slides, photo cover) - IKEA
IKEA makes you walk the whole store, then build the furniture yourself. Psychologists say the second part makes you value it more 🛋️

The path through an IKEA winds past room after room, so you keep walking and get reminded of things you'd forgotten.
Then comes the assembly. In a 2012 study, people who built an IKEA box were willing to pay significantly more for it than for one built by someone else.
The catch: it only works when you finish. When people failed to complete the task, the effect disappeared.

Would you rather buy it built or build it yourself? 👇
