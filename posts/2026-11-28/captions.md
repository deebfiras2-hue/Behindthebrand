## Carousel (7 slides, number cover, no photos) - PepsiCo vs Coca-Cola
Pepsi sells twice as much as Coke. Coke still earns more 🥤

In 2025 PepsiCo reported $93.9 billion in revenue, almost double Coca-Cola's $47.9 billion.
But Coke's reported operating profit was $13.8 billion vs PepsiCo's $11.5 billion, a margin of about 29% vs 12%.
Coke mostly sells concentrate to bottling partners. Pepsi also runs snacks (Frito-Lay) and much of its own bottling.
Fair print: one-offs skew the reported numbers. Adjusted, Coke's margin (31%) is still about double Pepsi's (about 16%).

Team Coke or team Pepsi? 👇
