## Carousel (6 slides, photo cover) - Delta x American Express
Amex paid Delta $8.2 billion in 2025. Delta's entire operating income was $5.8 billion ✈️

Delta's credit card partner is a money machine hiding behind the plane.
American Express issues the Delta SkyMiles cards, and cardholders earn miles on their spending. Amex pays Delta for it: $8.2 billion in 2025, up 11%.
Revenue isn't profit, but that one payment was bigger than the airline's whole operating income.
Delta now targets $10 billion a year within a few years.
