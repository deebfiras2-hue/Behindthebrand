Starbucks is secretly a bank that sells coffee. Right now, customers have roughly two billion dollars loaded onto Starbucks cards and its app. That's money Starbucks holds before you've bought a single latte... and it pays no interest on it. Even better, when people forget a balance, Starbucks books that unspent money as income. Over two hundred million dollars of it in fiscal twenty twenty-four. It's an interest-free loan from millions of coffee lovers. The lesson? Get paid first, deliver later. Would you load money on a coffee card? Follow Behind The Brand for more.
